**[[SCENARIO \# 01]{.underline}]{.mark}**

**The child opens the app and selects Scenario 1: Good Touch / Bad Touch
Recognition.**

Before anything starts, the **Checklist Screen** appears asking the
child to tick what they already know:

-   Do you know your home address? ✓

-   Do you know a parent\'s phone number? ✓

-   Do you know what a trusted adult is? ✗

-   Have you learned about body safety before? ✗

Then the simulation begins.

**SCENE 1: The Guide NPC Opens (Info Scene)**

The Guide NPC: a friendly character named something like \"Dost\";
appears and says:

*\"Assalam o Alaikum! I\'m here to help you. Something happened to you
that felt strange or uncomfortable. I want to understand. Can you tell
me; did someone touch you in a way that made you feel confused or
upset?\"*

**Interaction type: MCQ**

-   A\) Yes, someone touched me and it felt wrong

-   B\) I\'m not sure, it felt strange but I don\'t know

-   C\) Maybe, but they said it was normal

All three options lead forward; none are \"wrong\" here. The Guide NPC
validates whatever the child picks.

**SCENE 2: The NPC Situation Begins (Info Scene)**

Now the actual scenario loads. Background: a home or school setting. An
NPC; could be a relative, teacher, or family friend; appears. The scene
shows what happened:

*Uncle Hamza came to visit. He hugged you and then touched your
shoulder, back, and then your private area. He said \"This is just how I
show love to kids I like.\"*

The Guide NPC appears in a bubble:

*\"How did that make you feel?\"*

**Interaction type: MCQ**

-   A\) Confused; I\'m not sure if it was okay

-   B\) Uncomfortable and scared

-   C\) It felt wrong but he\'s a family elder so maybe it\'s okay

Each answer is accepted. The Guide NPC moves forward based on the
child\'s response; if C is selected, the Guide NPC gently challenges it:

*\"It\'s very common to feel that way. But let me tell you something
important; no adult should ever touch your private parts, no matter who
they are or what reason they give.\"*

**SCENE 3: Good Touch vs Bad Touch Teaching (Info Scene)**

The Guide NPC explains the rule; this is a read-only teaching scene:

*\"Here is something you must always remember:*

-   *A good touch makes you feel safe, warm, and happy; like a
    handshake, a pat on the back, or a hug you wanted.*

-   *A bad touch makes you feel confused, scared, uncomfortable, or
    wrong inside; especially if it involves your private parts.*

-   *Your private parts are the parts covered by your swimsuit. Nobody
    should touch them except a doctor with your parent present.\"*

**Interaction type: Info / Continue button**

**SCENE 4: What Did You Feel? (Slider)**

*\"Think about what Uncle Hamza did. On this scale; how did his touch
make you feel?\"*

Scale from 😊 Safe and happy → 😐 Confused → 😨 Scared and wrong

**Interaction type: Slider / Confidence Scale**

The child moves the slider. Whatever they choose, the Guide NPC
validates and moves forward. If they select \"confused\" or
\"scared/wrong\", the Guide NPC says:

*\"That feeling you had inside; that uncomfortable, wrong feeling; that
is your body\'s alarm. It is always telling you the truth. Trust it.\"*

**SCENE 5: What Should You Do? (MCQ Decision: First Critical Choice)**

*\"Uncle Hamza told you not to tell anyone. He said \'This is our
secret.\' What do you do?\"*

**Interaction type: MCQ**

-   A\) Keep the secret because he is a family elder ❌

-   B\) Tell Ammi or Baba immediately ✅

-   C\) Wait and see if it happens again ❌

**If the child picks A:**

Consequence scene appears; a coaching card:

*\"🔴 Secrets about touches are never okay to keep. Safe adults never
ask children to keep secrets about their body. When someone says
\'don\'t tell anyone\' about a touch --- that is the biggest sign that
something is wrong. You must tell.\"*

Guide NPC: *\"Let\'s try again. What would you do?\"* --- MCQ repeats

**If the child picks C:**

*\"🟡 Waiting can feel safer, but it allows the harm to continue. You
deserve to be safe right now --- not later. Let\'s think about what to
do immediately.\"*

**If the child picks B:**

*\"🟢 Yes! Telling a trusted adult immediately is always the right first
step. You are so brave. Let\'s see what happens next.\"*

**SCENE 6 --- Who is a Trusted Adult? (Drag to Rank)**

*\"Before you tell someone, you need to know who a trusted adult is. Put
these people in order --- who would you tell first?\"*

**Interaction type: Drag to Rank**

List to reorder:

1.  Ammi or Baba (parents)

2.  A teacher at school

3.  An older sibling

4.  Uncle Hamza\'s friend

Correct order: Parents first → Teacher → Older sibling → Uncle Hamza\'s
friend is NOT a trusted adult in this situation.

Guide NPC coaches after ranking:

*\"Your parents are always your first trusted adult. If a parent is not
available, a teacher, school counselor, or another trusted family member
is next. Never tell the person who hurt you or their close friends.\"*

**SCENE 7: Telling Ammi (Voice Input Scene)**

Background changes to home; living room. Ammi NPC appears.

*\"You went to Ammi. She looks at you and says: \'Beta, you look upset.
What happened? You can tell me anything.\'\"*

Guide NPC: *\"What would you say to Ammi? Tell her in your own words.\"*

**Interaction type: Voice Input (Paid) / Free Text (Free tier)**

The child speaks or types what happened. The n8n AI workflow evaluates:

-   Did they clearly describe what happened?

-   Did they name the person?

-   Did they describe the body part?

The Ammi NPC responds dynamically based on what the child said ---
acknowledging, asking follow-up questions like a real parent would.

**SCENE 8: Ammi\'s Response (Consequence + Info)**

Ammi NPC says:

*\"Beta, thank you for telling me. You did absolutely the right thing.
What happened to you was WRONG. It was not your fault. Not even a little
bit. Uncle Hamza should never have done that. I am going to make sure
you are safe.\"*

Consequence card appears:

*\"🌟 You told a trusted adult clearly and immediately. This is the
bravest and most important thing a child can do. Telling someone stops
the harm.\"*

Guide NPC adds:

*\"Notice how Ammi believed you immediately? Real trusted adults always
believe children about touches. If someone doesn\'t believe you --- tell
another trusted adult.\"*

**SCENE 9: What Happens Next? (MCQ)**

*\"Ammi wants to know what to do next. What should she do?\"*

**Interaction type: MCQ**

-   A\) Talk to Uncle Hamza privately and ask him to stop ❌

-   B\) Call the police or child protection helpline ✅

-   C\) Keep it in the family and handle it quietly ❌

**If A or C:** Coaching card explains why these are wrong ---
confronting the abuser alone can make things worse; keeping it quiet
protects the abuser not the child.

**If B:**

*\"🟢 Yes. Reporting to authorities protects you and prevents this from
happening to other children. This is not betrayal --- this is
protection.\"*

**SCENE 10: Final Guide NPC Message (Debrief Begins)**

Guide NPC final message:

*\"You did amazingly well. Before we see your score, I want to leave you
with three rules you must never forget:*\
*1. Your body belongs to you. Nobody has the right to touch your private
parts.*\
*2. No means no --- even with family elders.*\
*3. Always tell. Secrets about touches are never okay to keep.\"*

**The key design principle throughout:** ZeeshanGamer99 was never
presented as obviously dangerous, he was friendly, funny,
age-appropriate in his first three weeks, and used completely normal
conversation topics. The danger emerged gradually, exactly as real
online grooming works. Every wrong choice in this scenario exploited a
completely natural instinct, the desire to make threats stop, the fear
of parental reaction, the guilt of having talked to a stranger for
weeks, and every coaching card addressed that instinct directly rather
than simply labelling the choice as wrong. The child learns that online
safety is not about avoiding all online contact but about recognizing
specific patterns, preserving evidence, and immediately involving
trusted adults the moment those patterns appear.
