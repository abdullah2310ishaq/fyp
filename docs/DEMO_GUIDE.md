# Five-minute LifeIQ demo

1. Launch and show the offline/privacy notice.
2. Register with any local demo credentials and OTP `1234`.
3. Create a nickname/age/avatar profile.
4. Open S01, complete its readiness checklist, feeling slider and a coached unsafe choice.
5. Show mock voice/text disclosure and the non-blaming adult response.
6. Complete the story and show score, coins, badge and locked premium breakdown.
7. Open a locked story, pass the parent gate (`7 + 8 = 15`), and activate premium demo.
8. Show S04 multi-select, S02/S03 ranking, S06 grounding, Urdu/RTL and reduced motion.
9. Open Settings → grown-up demo panel (`9 + 7 = 16`) to demonstrate free/active/expired and reset.

No step needs connectivity, a real login, microphone, payment or personal information.
