# Safety and editorial log

## Applied revisions

- “Wrong” red-failure framing is replaced with “Let’s think again” coaching.
- SIS is displayed as a simulated practice score, never intelligence, diagnosis or real-world safety prediction.
- Emotional intensity, freezing, disclosure readiness, checklists, grounding and verbal fluency are not scored.
- Body-safety contact is described in text and never visually depicted.
- The adult response uses “appropriate local protection or police support” rather than presenting an unverified demo phone number as live.
- Voice is explicitly a fixed demo transcript; no microphone access or recording occurs.
- Text practice warns against real personal details and is not persisted.
- Parent subscription and demo-state controls use maths gates; all prices and billing are labelled simulated.
- S05–S08 content is labelled editorial expansion and is not attributed to `Scenario.md`.
